# Source Fidelity

Dungeon Crawler World Zero uses the Dungeon Crawler Carl novels as its default design reference. Working prototype systems remain in place, but a floor may not be presented as source-derived unless its blueprint identifies the supporting book and chapters.

## Labels

- `direct`: represented substantially as described in the source.
- `compressed_adaptation`: preserves the rule or consequence with smaller counts, shorter animations, or simplified geometry.
- `original_prologue`: original material used to connect or teach existing game systems.
- `original`: intentionally new content that is not attributed to the novels.

## Floor 2 direct elements

- A grid-like tutorial quadrant built from cinderblock passages.
- White flooring and orange lichen as its defining visual read.
- Six real-world dungeon days before collapse.
- Low-tier loot and build experimentation before Floor 3 classification.
- Brindle Grubs functioning as janitor mobs that feed on corpses.
- Feeding can lead to pupation and Brindled Vespa emergence.
- A neighborhood boss may violate the expected boundary of its chamber.

## Reference record

Primary reference: Matt Dinniman, *Dungeon Crawler Carl*, Book 1, Floor 2 arc (approximately Chapters 29–41; chapter numbering may vary by edition). The blueprint stores the specific chapters used during implementation. Unofficial indexes are used only to cross-check names and mechanics, never as a substitute for the novel.

## Floor 2 compressed elements

- One generated map represents one regional quadrant.
- Active janitor mobs are capped for browser and mobile performance.
- Each corpse spawns a small group rather than the source population range.
- Offline time advances deadlines and biological stages without simulating continuous combat.

## Review rule

Every new floor blueprint must list direct elements, compressed elements, and original additions before its mechanics are implemented.
